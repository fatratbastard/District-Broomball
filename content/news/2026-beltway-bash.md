Beltway Broomball Bash

The fourth annual Beltway Broomball Bash will take place on Saturday, June 6 in Reisterstown, Maryland. It is a one-day, co-rec draft tournament that mixes people from the DC and Baltimore broomball leagues. Teams try hard on the ice but treat it like a chill barbecue off the ice. 

Registration is now live at [4th Annual BBB Draft Tournament](https://www.charmcitybroomball.com/programs#:~:text=4th%20Annual%20BBB%20Draft%20Tournament%20%2D%20Annual%202026). Talk to AJ or Brett if you have  questions.

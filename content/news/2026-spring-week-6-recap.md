**Game 1: Pink Pony Club 1, I Choose You, Charizard 5** Yellow took an early lead when Hauke's shot from in-close was saved, but he was first to the rebound and flipped it in. Hauke scored again, eight minutes into the game, when Bolyard found him cutting backdoor in transition and he converted the chance to make it 2-0. Just before halftime, Yurasko Sr. extended Yellow's lead to 3-0 to after a scramble in front of Pink's net saw Guggenheim save three or four shots before Yurasko scooped the ball into the top of the net. Early in the second half, Pink had an offensive sequence of several consecutive shots from outside the circles, with Conway eventually burying one to make it a 3-1 game. Midway through the second half, Klueber scored from the wing on a shot through traffic to make it 4-1 for Yellow. Pink committed everyone forward chasing the game late, and racked up shots on goal; but the final scoring play was Hauke tallying his third for Yellow on a counter with one minute left in the game. 

**Game 2: Shadow Kirby's Dream Land 1, Don't Stan So Klos To Me 1** The second game of the night had just as many shots on goal as the early game but more saves for the goalies. Midway through the first period, a bit of pinball in the neutral zone landed the ball on Bowman's stick and he sprinted into the Blue end and ripped in a shot to put Black ahead 1-0. That score held until halftime. In the second half, Black upped the pressure but it was Blue who scored against the run of play. With ten minutes remaining in the game, Getches played a ball off the boards to a streaking Kleven, who sprinted into the Black end with a defender harrying him and popped in a shot to tie the game 1-1. There were more scoring chances over the final ten minutes, but neither team was able to find the back of the net again.

**Three Stars:** Nathan Hauke, AJ Bolyard, Connor Rattey

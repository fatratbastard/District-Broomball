**Game 1: Broomdock Saints 3, Mad Mach: Fuschia Road 4** Black scored three minutes into the game when Flesch was first to a rebound and roofed it over the goalie's shoulder for a 1-0 lead. Seven minutes later, Fletcher slung a pass from the low boards to Ault in the middle and he whipped it in off the back post to put Black ahead 2-0. On the next shift, Kleven picked off a breakout pass, drove across the middle of the ice, and shot into the top left corner for a 3-0 Black advantage. Pink got one back just before halftime, when Laux shot into a defender, got his own rebound, and buried his second attempt to make the score 3-1. Ten minutes into the second half, Angotti picked up a loose ball, carried it into the Black zone, and scored on a well-placed shot from distance, 3-2. One minute later, Getches sent the ball up to Kamphaus in transition and he tallied from the left dot to tie the game for Pink at 3-3. And in the next minute, Pink completed their comeback when Kamphaus wristed in a rebound after a Yurasko shot deflected to him near the back post, 3-4. Black didn't give up anything else after that Pink scoring flurry, but they also weren't able to find a goal to even the score over the game's final ten minutes.

**Three Stars:** Tim Kamphaus, Hannah Flesch, Dave Laux

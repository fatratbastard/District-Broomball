**Game 1: Yellow Team 0, Blue Team 4**
After ten scoreless minutes to begin the game, Fierst pushed the ball up to Garrity for a breakaway and he lifted the ball over the goalie's shoulder to put Blue ahead 1-0. That score held through halftime and nearly fourteen minutes of the second half, up until Bowman ran down an almost-icing, dribbled into the middle, and blasted in a shot for a 2-0 Blue lead. Three minutes later, Pawlo shot from the point through traffic and beat the goalie five-hole to put Blue up 3-0. Then in the final minute of the game, with Yellow's goalie pulled, Bowman collected a bouncing ball and slid it home to ice the game for Blue. Yellow kept pace with Blue for shots on goal, but Joyce stopped everything he saw to earn a shutout for Blue. 

**Game 2: Pink Team 1, Black Team 0** 
Both teams' defenses and goalies outplayed the two offenses in this game's first half, where the closest thing to a goal was a one-on-goalie breakaway for Black that Guggenheim turned away. Five minutes into the second half, Ulerich passed to Bay in the slot and she one-timed the ball for a pretty goal to put Pink ahead 1-0. Black upped the pressure from there, outshooting Pink in the second half 7 to 2, but despite a few good scoring chances they were unable to put the ball into the back of Pink's net.

**Three Stars**
Simon Guggenheim, Mike Pawlo, Katie Bay

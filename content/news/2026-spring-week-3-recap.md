**Game 1: Don't Stan So Klos To Me 3, Shadow Kirby's Dream Land 2** -- Kleven opened the scoring for Blue seven minutes into the game when a shot from the left point caromed to him and he banged it in for a 1-0 lead. Five minutes later, Pawlo extended Blue's lead to 2-0 after carrying the ball to the right circle on a fast break and firing a shot just inside the far post. Two minutes after halftime, Hopkins received a pass in the slot and slid it in to put Blue ahead 3-0. Black would go on to outshoot Blue in the second half, but didn't break through with a goal until, with eight minutes remaining in the game, Bowman picked off a pass and found K. Kirby open in the middle of the ice for a big shot that found the back of the net. Then, with under three minutes left, K. Kirby stole the ball at mid-ice and carried it beyond the Blue defense to score and make it a one-goal game, 3-2. Black pulled their goalie late, but couldn't get one more tally to tie the game. 

**Game 2: Pink Pony Club 3, I Choose You, Charizard 2** -- Yellow had the first six shots on goal in this game, but Pink got the first shot that went in when Ulerich stole an outlet pass in the Yellow zone and scored before the defense could recover to put Pink ahead 1-0 midway through the first half. Pink added another with six minutes left in the half after forcing a turnover in the Yellow zone; the ball eventually found Ulerich whose shot from the slot deflected in. Then, just before halftime, Pink had a two-on-none breakaway chance that resulted in a Kamphaus tap-in, 3-0. Like the Black team in the early game, Yellow regrouped and outshot Pink in the second half. They too scored with eight minutes remaining, this time with Yellow pressure forcing a turnover in front of the Pink net and Hauke lifting it in to make the score 3-1. Yellow pulled their goalie late, and with thirty seconds to go and an extra attacker, Angotti shot into traffic from the left point and the ball went past everyone on Pink, goalie included, to make it 3-2. But Pink didn't allow any more scoring chances at the end. 

**Three Stars:** Joe Ulerich, Mike Pawlo, Kasey Kirby

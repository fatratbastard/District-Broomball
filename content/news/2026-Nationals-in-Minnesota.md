Best of luck to those of you heading out to Minneapolis for the national broomball tournament later this week! 

Following the tournament online is a bit haphazard, but this twitter feed should be posting score updates [@AEBroomball](https://x.com/AEBroomball) and this facebook page will stream games [Broomball Central LIVE](https://www.facebook.com/BroomballCentralLIVE/).

Who should you be rooting for? Our league will have people playing for Bipartisans (co-rec A), Corruption (mens B), Wicked Sisters (womens B), Heroines (womens B), Legion BC (mens A), and Nebraska (mens C).

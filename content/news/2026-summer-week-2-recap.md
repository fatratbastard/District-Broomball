**Game 1: Pink Team 3, Black Team 5**
After fifteen scoreless minutes to start the game, Kamphaus put Pink ahead 1-0 on a big rip from the outside. Black answered one minute later, with a nifty passing sequence that found Kleven open on the back post for a goal, 1-1. Kleven added another two minutes before halftime when he jumped a Pink passing lane and converted the breakaway, 2-1. Less than a minute before halftime, Pink tied the game at 2-2 when Fajman found Kamphaus cutting back door. Early in the second half, Garrity was first to a rebound in front of the Pink net and scored to give Black a 3-2 lead. Kamphaus completed his hat trick a few minutes later, with a low shot through traffic from the right circle to tie the game again, 3-3. Black reclaimed the lead with ten minutes remaining, when Keane fired the ball home on a feed from Flesch. Pink wasn't able to pull even down the stretch; an empty-netter from Ault was the game's final tally.

**Three Stars:** Tim Kamphaus, Matthew Keane, Adam Kleven

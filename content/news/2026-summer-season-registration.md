The Summer 2026 season will be at Tucker Road on Tuesday nights with games starting at 9:00 p.m. Our season begins on **May 26**. It's an eight-game guarantee and league dues are $120.

More details are at [Register for Next Season](https://capitolbroomball.wixsite.com/capitolbroomball/register-for-next-season).

Summer is a little complicated. Each week one team is on bye. When you register, you'll be asked for your planned weeks out-of-town, then placed on a team so that your travel matches up with your team's bye weeks.

Sign up by 1) completing the player questionnaire and then 2) submitting a payment using the PayPal button. If you'd rather pay with cash or check, you still need to fill out the player questionnaire.

The deadline to register is Sunday, May 24 at 7 p.m.

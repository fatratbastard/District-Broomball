**Game 1: Broomdock Saints 3, Release the Schneider Cut 0** The first half was nearly scoreless, but with thirty seconds left Kamphaus rushed up the right boards and centered the ball to Conway (whose initial pass to Kamphaus sparked the counter) for a Black tally. Blue had a significant lead in shots on goal at halftime, but were down 1-0 on the scoreboard. Four minutes into the second half, Kamphaus stole the ball, rushed down the ice, had his initial sliding shot saved, but got a fortunate bounce off a lower extremity and the ball rolled across the line. Blue continued to control possession and hit the goal posts a few times, but never found the back of the net. With two minutes to go and Blue pressing, Garrity picked up a loose ball, ran down the ice and then slammed the brakes, scoring from close range after two Blue defenders slid past. Black earned our summer season's first shutout with the 3-0 victory.

**Three Stars:** Simon Guggenheim, Greg Conway, Dan Negron

**Game 1: Release the Schneider Cut 4, Broomdock Saints 2** Ten minutes into the game, the Blue team pulled off a nifty passing sequence on a rush into the zone, from Eger to Schneider to Sarnell to Eger and in, to take a 1-0 lead. Three minutes later, Bowman shot wide from the left dot but Schneider  redirected the ball into the net at the far post, 2-0 for Blue. Five minutes after halftime, Bowman received a stretch pass, shimmied past a defender and dragged the ball wide of the goalie to score, 3-0. Ault answered for Black a few minutes later, smashing in a shot from the left circle to bring his team back to 3-1. With ten minutes left, Langrock fed a pass into the low slot from the right side and Bowman poked it through to make it 4-1 Blue. In the game's final minute, with Black on a penalty kill, Garrity picked off a pass, ran the length of the ice, missed the initial breakaway chance, but collected his own rebound and slid the ball in around two Blue defenders and the goalie.

**Three Stars:** John Bowman, Nathan Centofanti, Alex Ault

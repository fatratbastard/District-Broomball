We’ll kick off our end‑of‑season celebration with a picnic and outdoor games at [Kenilworth Aquatic Gardens](https://maps.app.goo.gl/X7NRY4Lx289KYH7i6) in NE DC on Saturday, May 2 at 2:00 PM. Partners and kids are absolutely welcome. The park is free to enter (National Park Service). There’s a parking lot on site, bike paths nearby, and it’s about a 15‑minute walk from the Deanwood stop on the Orange Line.

A few days before the event, we’ll send out our exact meetup location within the park.

We’ll bring Spikeball, cornhole, and Kan Jam, but feel free to bring any other yard games you enjoy, along with snacks and drinks. A note on alcohol: DC generally prohibits open alcohol in public outdoor spaces unless specifically permitted. Kenilworth does not list alcohol as allowed, so if you bring drinks, please be discreet and use good judgment.

We’ll also be announcing several year-long awards during the picnic. Are you a first or second team all-star? Are you just a star? Find out on May 2!

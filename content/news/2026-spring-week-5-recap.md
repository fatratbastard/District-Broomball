**Game 1: Shadow Kirby's Dream Land 6, I Choose You, Charizard 1**
Yurasko Jr. struck first, five minutes into the game, knocking home a rebound from close range to put Yellow ahead 1-0. Black tied the game halfway through the first period when Bowman found K. Kirby in space in transition and his shot found the back of the net. He scored again with seven minutes remaining in the period after stealing a breakout pass and scoring before the defense could recover. Just before halftime, Negron put the ball into a crowd in front of the Yellow net and Eger took possession and scored to put Black up 3-1. Yellow would tally more shots on goal than Black in the second half, but the period's first goal was from K. Kirby on a shot from the blue line that spun in past the near post to make it 4-1. Yellow pulled their goalie with ten minutes remaining, which ultimately left the net empty for goals from K. Kirby (after stealing the ball at the red line) and Negron (upon clearing the ball from his own goal line). 

**Game 2: Pink Pony Club 4, Don't Stan So Klos To Me 3**
Four minutes into the game, Hopkins sliced through the slot and drove in a low shot to put Blue ahead 1-0. Then two minutes later, Leonard cleaned up a rebound to put Blue up 2-0. Just before halftime, Ulerich stole a pass, carried the ball into the Blue zone, and scored from the left circle to bring Pink back within a goal, 2-1. Pink tied the game a few minutes into the second half when Conway dribbled along the boards into the corner, curled into the middle, and snuck in a shot to make it 2-2. Three minutes later, Fajman gave Pink the lead, shooting first-time on a feed into the middle from Langrock. With five minutes left in the game, Blue worked the ball up ice to Kleven who flung in a backhand to make it 3-3. With ninety seconds left, Ulerich scored the game-winner when Fajman found him open in the Blue zone with time to shoot. 

**Three Stars:** Dan Negron, Paul Fajman, Connor Rattey

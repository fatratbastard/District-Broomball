**Game 1: Release the Schneider Cut 1, Mad Mach: Fuschia Road 4** Halfway through the first period, Bowman rushed into the zone along the right boards and centered the ball to Nudi for a one-timer. Her shot was saved off to the left circle, where Bay was waiting to smash a shot into the top of the net for a 1-0 Blue lead. Pink tied the score 90 seconds before halftime when Kraft picked up a loose ball in the offensive zone, passed to Matroniano, and he drove in a shot from the left circle. Then 30 seconds before halftime, Yurasko gave Pink the lead when his low shot from the right dot deflected upward off a Blue defender and beyond the goalie's reach. And 30 seconds after halftime, Yurasko scored again to make it 3-1, this time sliding in a shot from between the hash marks after collecting the rebound from a blocked Shalaty shot attempt. To that point, Blue had doubled Pink's shot total but Pink scored three goals in a two-minute surge. The two defenses held both offenses in check over the next twenty minutes. Hopkins walked in an empty-netter with three seconds left on the clock to make it a 4-1 final score.

**Three Stars:** William Yurasko, Katie Bay, Jess Kraft

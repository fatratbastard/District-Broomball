**Game 1: Pink Team 2, Blue Team 2**
Ten minutes into the game, Eger slung a pass off the right boards to an open Bowman cutting into the slot and he converted the chance to put Blue ahead 1-0. Four minutes later, Blue doubled their lead when Schneider collected the ball behind the Pink net and found Bay open in front who punched in the tally. Shots on goal were lopsided in Blue's favor during the first half as well. But one minute into the second period, Angotti got the ball at the top of the right circle with his back to net, turned and shot in one motion, and kissed the ball off the near post and in to put Pink on the board, 2-1. A long stretch of scoreless play followed, up until Lintner fired a ball into traffic from the right boards and an errant Blue foot deflected the ball into the goal to make it 2-2 for Pink. Neither team was able to score in the game's final six minutes. 

**Three Stars:** Katie Bay, Vince Angotti, Brad Lintner

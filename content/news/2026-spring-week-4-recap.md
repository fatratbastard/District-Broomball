**Game 1: Don't Stan So Klos To Me 1, I Choose You, Charizard 4** 
Eight minutes into the game, Yurasko Jr. one-timed a shot to complete a two-on-one rush into the zone and put Yellow ahead 1-0. Six minutes later, Campbell picked off a Blue clearance and scored from the point to put Yellow up 2-0. Then just before halftime, D. Laux collected a rebound by the right dot and found Yurasko Jr. open in front of the net for another Yellow tally. Early in the second half, Angotti scored the prettiest goal of the night, spinning and scooping a low pass into the top of the net from a tough angle, 4-0. Blue kept even with Yellow for shots on goal in the second half, and midway through the period Leonard shot off the post with the rebound kicking out to Rattey who rifled in a shot to wipe Yellow's shutout bid. 

**Game 2: Pink Pony Club 0, Shadow Kirby's Dream Land 5**
On the first shift of the game, K. Kirby received a pass in the left circle, drifted toward the middle, and found a window to shoot and put Black up 1-0. A few minutes later, Oehlschlager sprung K. Kirby on a breakaway and he converted the chance to make it 2-0 Black. Both defenses held up over the remaining sixteen minutes of the first half. On the first shift of the second half, Black passed their way through the Pink defense and L. Kirby scored from close range to set their lead at 3-0. With ten minutes to go, K. Kirby snagged a breakout pass and immediately blasted in a shot to make it 4-0. Then with four minutes to go, Bowman carried the ball into open space near the right dot and fired it home to put a cherry on top of Black's first win of the season.

**Three Stars:**
Mike Campbell, Lauren Kirby, William Yurasko Jr.

**Game 1: I Choose You, Charizard 3, Shadow Kirby's Dream Land 2**
Just one goal in the first half of this game, as the defenses limited shots from both teams. But with six minutes left before half,  Angotti stole a pass in the Black zone and shot quickly to put Yellow ahead 1-0. Five minutes into the second half, Sarnell collected the ball just outside the Black crease and punched it in to put Yellow ahead 2-0. Then with six minutes left, Yurasko Jr. put the ball into a scrum in front of the net and the result was a goal for Yurasko Sr. to make it 3-0 for Yellow. With three minutes remaining, K. Kirby blasted a shot from the left dot off the post, then off the goalie's back, and into the net to put Black on the board. On the next faceoff, he won the ball forward, bolted up the ice, and scored to make it a one-goal game for Black. Still, despite pulling their goalie for the final two minutes, Black wasn't able to get one more late tally to tie the game.

**Game 2: Pink Pony Club 2, Don't Stan So Klos To Me 2**
Blue did everything well in the first half except score goals. Despite a big discrepancy in shots on goal, Pink's defense bent without breaking, and the teams reached halftime tied 0-0. Five minutes into the second half, Rattey stole a pass in the neutral zone and passed to Kleven who passed to a cutting Fletcher and he scored the breakaway chance to put Blue up 1-0. Five minutes later, Conway stole the ball in the neutral zone, traded a few passes with Ulerich in transition, and Ulerich scored from close range to tie the game for Pink 1-1. Blue scored one minute later, unassisted as Kleven stole a breakout pass, rushed the net and lifted his shot over the goalie's shoulder. With thirty seconds remaining, a blocked Blue shot kicked out to Ulerich and he got a shot off just after crossing into the Blue zone on the left; Bay gathered the rebound at the right dot and her follow-up attempt from there floated in to tie the game 2-2.

**Three Stars**
Greg Conway, William Yurasko Sr., Adam Kleven

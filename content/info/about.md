The District Broomball League has been running competitive recreational broomball in the DMV area since 2010. 

## Our Mission

We provide a fun, safe, and competitive broomball environment for adult recreational players. Whether you are a seasoned veteran or picking up a broom for the first time, the District Broomball is the place to play.

## Season Structure

Barring a crippling pandemic District Broomball has 4 seasons each year. Each season runs roughly 8 weeks (this can change depending on the number of teams each season). Teams play a full round-robin schedule before a single-elimination playoff. Championship banquets (i.e. beer in the parking lot) and trophy presentations follow each season.
